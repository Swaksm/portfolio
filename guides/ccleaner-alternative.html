A free, open-source alternative to CCleaner

CCleaner is the best-known Windows cleaner, but it is a commercial product with a paid Pro version and a registry cleaner. If you want a tool that is free, open and explains what it deletes, here are the options.

Why look for an alternative

  • CCleaner is a commercial product (Gen Digital, the group behind Avast and Norton): the free version is limited and regularly offers the paid version.
  • It includes a registry cleaner. Microsoft does not support registry cleaning tools, and they free nothing that matters.
  • You often do not know why an item is offered for cleaning, or what you lose if you clean it.

What Kleaner does differently

  • It explains: every large file gets a verdict (safe, clean with its own tool, uninstall, breaks something, your files, system, unknown), with what happens if you delete it. With no rule, it says « unknown » rather than guessing.
  • Nothing is ticked in advance, and you can preview the exact list of files before anything is deleted.
  • Your personal files only go to the Recycle Bin. Permanent deletion is limited to a public list of caches.
  • It never cleans the registry, never touches passwords, cookies, sessions or history.
  • No account, no ads, no telemetry, no network connection (the update check is optional and off by default).
  • Free and open source (MIT): the code is on GitHub, and the exe is built from it by GitHub Actions.

What CCleaner does and Kleaner does not

To be fair: Kleaner runs on Windows 10 and 11 only, it does not clean the registry (on purpose), and it has no automatic scheduled cleaning. Its exe is not signed yet, so Windows shows a SmartScreen warning the first time. If you need one of those, CCleaner or the tools below may suit you better.

Other free options

  • Storage Sense, built into Windows (Settings › System › Storage): automatic cleaning of temporary files and the Recycle Bin. Simple, but it explains little.
  • BleachBit: open source, Windows and Linux, many cleaners. Powerful, aimed at people who know what they are ticking.
  • Kleaner: for those who want to understand what takes the space before deleting it, including developer, gamer and AI folders (node_modules, Docker, Steam, Ollama models…).

Switching from CCleaner

  • Download Kleaner (installer or portable version) and run a scan: you see your drives and what can be freed.
  • Clean the « safe » items first, then decide on the others one by one.
  • Uninstall CCleaner from Settings › Apps if you no longer need it.

Questions

Is Kleaner really free?

Yes. Kleaner is free and open source under the MIT license, with no paid version, no ads and no account.

Does Kleaner clean the registry?

No, on purpose. Cleaning the registry frees nothing useful and can break programs; Microsoft does not support registry cleaners. Kleaner only writes to the registry when you ask it to turn off a startup item or remove a dead program entry, and saves it first.

Why does Windows warn me when I launch Kleaner?

The exe is not signed with a paid certificate yet, so SmartScreen shows « Windows protected your PC ». Click More info then Run anyway. The code is public and the exe is built from it by GitHub Actions; its SHA-256 hash lets you check you got that build.
