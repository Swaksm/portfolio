Kleaner.

A free Windows cleaner that tells you what you can delete — and what breaks if you do.

RustTauriWindows 10 / 11MIT
Try it → GitHub ↗

Why

Most cleaners either delete blindly or show a list of sizes without saying what is safe. Kleaner explains first: every large file and folder gets a verdict — safe, clean with its tool, uninstall, breaks something, your files, system or unknown — with what it is, what happens if you delete it and how to free the space properly.

The verdicts come from 108 hand-written rules. With no rule, the verdict is “unknown”: Kleaner never guesses.

What it does

  • Scans the drives you choose: free space, largest files and folders, breakdown by type, disk map. In administrator mode on NTFS it reads the file table directly — about 4 to 10 times faster (2 s instead of 8 to 21 s on a 420 GB disk).
  • Cleanup: 45 built-in items plus about 1,100 from the community catalog, each saying what it removes and what changes for you.
  • Knows developer, gamer and AI machines: old node_modules, .venv or target folders (with the command that rebuilds them), Docker and WSL disks, Ollama, LM Studio and Hugging Face models, game launchers.
  • Programs: real size on disk, last launch date, leftovers of uninstalled programs. Startup items and boot times, duplicates, a photo & video browser.
  • A read-only health & security check-up, and an offline AI report — a Markdown summary to paste into the AI of your choice. 6 languages, light and dark themes.

Safe by design

  • Your files only go to the Recycle Bin, and the log can restore them.
  • Permanent deletion is limited to a public list of caches, enforced in Rust and covered by tests.
  • Nothing is ticked in advance, and “Preview the exact list” shows every file before anything is deleted.
  • No network, no telemetry, no account. It never touches passwords, cookies, sessions or history — and never “cleans” the registry.

Under the hood

A Rust backend with a Tauri interface, and a knowledge base in JSON (rules, cleaners and a filtered community catalog). Shipped as a portable exe built by GitHub Actions, with a SHA-256 hash to check the download — also installable with Scoop.
