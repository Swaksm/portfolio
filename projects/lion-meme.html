Lion Meme.

“You think you're a lion… but you're actually this.” A meme generator built on the gap between the image we project and reality.

ReactTypeScriptViteCanvas
Visit site ↗ GitHub ↗

How it works

  • Pick a format: square or rectangle.
  • Choose your lion — two majestic presets, or your own picture.
  • Add your “real nature”: the far less glorious photo.
  • Preview the meme live, then download it or share it in one click.

Under the hood

A single-page app in React 19 and TypeScript, built with Vite. The meme is composed directly in the browser on a canvas, so the pictures stay on your device. Hosted on Netlify.
