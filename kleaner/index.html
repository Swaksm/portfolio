Frequently asked questions

How do I free up disk space on Windows 11?

Open Kleaner and click “Clean risk-free”: it ticks the useless caches and temporary files, shows you the list, then deletes them. Then look at your largest files and the leftovers of old programs, each with its explanation.

Is it really free?

Yes. No paid version, no ads, no account. The source code is public.

Is Kleaner a CCleaner alternative?

Yes, a free one with no ads. The difference: Kleaner explains every file before deleting it, never “cleans” the registry and sends nothing over the Internet.

Is it safe?

Nothing is ticked in advance. Permanent deletion is limited to caches that rebuild themselves; your personal files only go to the Recycle Bin and can be restored.

Why does Windows show a warning?

Kleaner is not signed with a paid certificate yet, so Windows does not recognise it. Click “More info”, then “Run anyway”.

Installer or portable version?

The installer adds Kleaner to the Start menu and to Windows apps. The portable version is a single file that runs without installing anything.

How do I uninstall it?

Windows Settings → Apps → Kleaner → Uninstall. Or, in Kleaner: Settings → Remove Kleaner.
